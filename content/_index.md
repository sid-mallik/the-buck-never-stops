---
title: The buck never stops
description: An agent's entanglement with the market economy is not a choice.
---

An agent’s entanglement with the market economy is not a choice. Instead, the agent pursues a direction received through the arrows of time that the markets nurture. Macro-security has a larger, intangible presence that weighs upon the agent’s expectations. This space concerns the moral compass that embodies the possible sway, which, when constrained, induces divergence. The buck never stops.
